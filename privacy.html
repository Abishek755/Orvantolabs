Privacy Policy

What data Vanto Connect and orvantolabs.in process, why, how it is protected, and how to have it deleted.

In short

  • We process Zoho CRM and Meta WhatsApp data only to run Vanto Connect for you.
  • We never sell, rent or trade your data, or your customers’ data.
  • Data is encrypted in transit, and access tokens are encrypted at rest.
  • To delete your data, email info@orvantolabs.in. See data deletion.

01Who we are

Orvanto Labs (“we”, “us”), based in Kovilpatti, Tamil Nadu, India, operates the website orvantolabs.in and the Vanto Connect product, a native WhatsApp Cloud API integration, team inbox and bot automation app for Zoho CRM.

For information you give us directly (for example, through our contact form or when you buy a plan or license), we decide how it is used. For the data your business processes through Vanto Connect — such as your customers’ WhatsApp messages and CRM records — your business is in control, and we process that data on your behalf and on your instructions.

02Data we collect from you

  • Contact form: your name, email address, optional WhatsApp number, the service you’re interested in and your message.
  • Purchases: your name, email address, country and order details. Card and payment details are handled by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Support and onboarding: emails and information you share with us to set up or support your workspace.
  • Technical data: basic server logs (such as IP address, browser type and timestamps) kept by our hosting provider for security and reliability.

03Zoho CRM data

When you authorize Vanto Connect in your Zoho CRM organization, we access only the permissions you approve, and only to provide the Product. Depending on the features you use, this can include:

  • Your Zoho CRM organization identifier and the names and email addresses of CRM users who use Vanto Connect (for chat assignment).
  • Lead and Contact fields that your bot flows read or write — for example name, email, phone number and other fields you map.
  • Conversation and WhatsApp voice call logs written to CRM records.
  • Zoho Books quotes and invoices that you choose to deliver over WhatsApp.

This data stays in your Zoho CRM organization. We store only what is needed to run your bot flows, inbox and sync.

04Meta WhatsApp Cloud API webhook data

Vanto Connect connects to your own Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API account. Through Meta’s API and webhooks we receive and process:

  • Your WhatsApp Business Account and phone number identifiers, and the access credentials you grant.
  • Messages and media sent and received through your number, including sender phone numbers and WhatsApp profile names.
  • Message status updates (such as sent, delivered and read) and timestamps.
  • WhatsApp Business voice call metadata used for call logging.
  • Click-to-WhatsApp ad referral information, used for lead attribution.
  • Spend data from Meta’s pricing_analytics, shown by conversation category (Marketing, Utility, Authentication, Service).

05How we use data

  • To provide Vanto Connect: routing messages to your team inbox, running your bot flows, validating answers, syncing Zoho CRM fields, logging calls, sending broadcasts and Zoho Books documents, and showing Meta spend.
  • To complete onboarding, provide support and respond to your inquiries.
  • To process payments and keep billing records.
  • To keep the Product secure, prevent spam and abuse, and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not use your WhatsApp conversations or CRM data for advertising.

06Zero data resale

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data — not yours, and not that of the people who message your business. We do not share Zoho CRM or WhatsApp data with advertisers or data brokers.

07Service providers

We share data only with providers that help us operate, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • Hosting and infrastructure providers that run our website and Product.
  • Payment provider that processes subscription and license payments.
  • Form handling (Formspree) for messages sent through our website contact form.
  • Meta and Zoho, as part of the Product: data moves between Vanto Connect and your own Meta and Zoho accounts, under their own privacy terms.

We may also disclose data if required by law, or as part of a business transfer, in which case this policy will continue to apply.

08Encryption & security

  • All data is encrypted in transit using TLS (HTTPS).
  • Sensitive credentials, such as Meta and Zoho access tokens, are encrypted at rest.
  • Access to customer data is limited to authorized Orvanto Labs personnel who need it to operate the Product or provide support you request.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your data, we will notify you without undue delay and as required by law.

09Retention

We keep Product data for as long as your Vanto Connect workspace is active. When you ask us to delete it, or your workspace is closed, we delete it as described below. Contact form messages are kept only as long as needed to respond and follow up. We keep billing records for as long as tax and accounting laws require.

10Data deletion

You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

  1. Email info@orvantolabs.in with the subject “Data deletion request”, from the email address linked to your account where possible.
  2. Include your Zoho CRM organization name and/or your WhatsApp Business phone number, so we can find your data.
  3. We will confirm your request, verify that it comes from you, and delete the data we hold within 30 days, then confirm by email. Billing records we must keep by law are excluded.

Disconnecting access. You can stop all further data flow at any time by uninstalling Vanto Connect from Zoho CRM and removing its access in your Meta Business settings.

If you messaged a business that uses Vanto Connect, that business controls your conversation data. You can ask them to delete it, or email us and we will pass your request to them and help them act on it.

Data stored in your own Zoho CRM and Meta accounts is also governed by Zoho’s and Meta’s own deletion tools and policies.

11Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have the right to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and raise a grievance. To exercise any of these rights, email info@orvantolabs.in. Grievances can be sent to the same address, marked “Grievance”, and we will respond within the time required by law.

12Cookies, children & international transfers

Cookies. orvantolabs.in does not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics trackers. Fonts and scripts are loaded from Google Fonts and cdnjs, which receive standard request data such as your IP address.

Children. Our website and Product are for businesses and are not directed at anyone under 18.

International transfers. Our service providers may process data outside India. Where they do, we take reasonable steps to make sure your data stays protected.

13Changes & contact

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here with a new effective date and, for material changes, notify customers by email.
