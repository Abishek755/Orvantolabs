  • Smart Question validation for Name, Email, Phone and Number

Questions

Before you buy.

Anything else? Email info@orvantolabs.in — we respond within 24 hours.

Do I need my own Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API account?

Yes. Vanto Connect uses a Bring Your Own Cloud (BYOC) model: you connect your own Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API account, and Meta bills you directly.

Does Orvanto Labs add a markup to Meta conversation charges?

No — 0% markup. You pay Meta directly at official Meta rates, and Vanto Connect shows your real-time spend by conversation category: Marketing, Utility, Authentication and Service.

What’s the difference between monthly plans and Founding Lifetime Access?

Starter, Growth and Pro are monthly subscriptions billed in INR or USD, each with its own features and limits. Founding Lifetime Access is a one-time payment — $399 USD (Global) or ₹14,999 INR (India) — for lifetime access to the full Pro feature suite.

Can I cancel a monthly plan?

Yes, at any time. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and you keep access until the end of the month you’ve already paid for. See the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

What are the Founding Lifetime limits?

Each lifetime license includes up to 10 Zoho CRM users, 10,000 automated messages per month and 10 active bot flows. Full details are in our Terms of Service.

What is the difference between the Global and India lifetime licenses?

Only the billing currency. Both include the identical full feature suite and the same fair-use limits; the India license is billed in INR for Indian Zoho CRM teams.

Can I get a refund?

If technical onboarding cannot be completed, you can request a refund within 7 days of your first payment. Once Zoho/Meta workspace activation has been delivered — and for monthly renewals — payments are non-refundable. See the Refund & Cancellation Policy.
