Refund & Cancellation Policy

How to cancel a Vanto Connect plan, when you can get a refund, when you can’t, and how to ask.

Effective 23 November 2026 Monthly plans: cancel anytime Support: info@orvantolabs.in

On this page

  1. What this covers
  2. Cancelling a monthly plan
  3. When you can get a refund
  4. When a refund isn’t available
  5. How to request a refund
  6. How refunds are paid
  7. Contact

In short

  • Monthly plans: cancel anytime. You keep access until the end of the paid month; we don’t refund partial months.
  • 7-day refund window on your first payment if technical onboarding cannot be completed.
  • Non-refundable once Zoho/Meta workspace activation has been delivered, and for monthly renewals.
  • Support, cancellation and refund requests: info@orvantolabs.in.

01What this covers

This policy applies to all Vanto Connect purchases from Orvanto Labs:

PlanPriceBilling
Starter, Growth, ProAs shown on our pricing section (INR or USD)Monthly subscription, renews until cancelled
Global Launch License (Founding Lifetime Access)$399 USDOne-time payment

Vanto Connect is delivered digitally through technical onboarding and activation in your Zoho CRM workspace.

02Cancelling a monthly plan

  • You can cancel a Starter, Growth or Pro plan at any time by emailing info@orvantolabs.in with the subject “Cancel subscription”, from the email address used for the purchase.
  • Cancelling stops the next renewal. You won’t be charged again.
  • You keep access until the end of the month you have already paid for. We don’t refund unused days of a partial month.

Launch licenses (Founding Lifetime Access) are one-time payments, so there is no recurring charge to cancel. Whatever your plan, you can stop using Vanto Connect at any time by uninstalling it from Zoho CRM and removing its access in your Meta Business settings. To ask us to delete your data afterwards, follow the steps in our Privacy Policy.

03When you can get a refund

You can request a full refund within 7 days of your first payment — for any monthly plan or lifetime license — if technical onboarding cannot be completed. For example, if a technical issue prevents Vanto Connect from being connected to your Zoho CRM organization or your Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API account, and we are unable to resolve it.

Before approving a refund, we will work with you to try to complete onboarding. If it can’t be completed, and you asked within 7 days of your first payment, you receive that payment back in full.

04When a refund isn’t available

  • After activation: once Zoho/Meta workspace activation has been delivered — meaning Vanto Connect is activated in your Zoho CRM workspace and connected to your Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API account — payments are non-refundable.
  • Monthly renewals: renewal payments are non-refundable. Cancel before your renewal date to avoid the next charge.
  • Partial months: we don’t refund unused days after you cancel.
  • After 7 days: refund requests made more than 7 days after your first payment.
  • Meta charges: WhatsApp conversation fees are billed by Meta directly to your Meta account, not by Orvanto Labs, so we cannot refund them. Contact Meta about those charges.
  • Policy violations: plans suspended or terminated for breaching our Terms of Service, including the anti-spam rules.

05How to request a refund

  1. Email info@orvantolabs.in with the subject “Refund request”.
  2. Include your order ID, the email address used for the purchase, the purchase date, and a short description of the onboarding issue.
  3. We aim to reply within 24 hours and will confirm whether the refund is approved.

06How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method, in the original currency, through our payment provider. Once the refund is issued, the time it takes to appear depends on your bank or card issuer, and is typically 5–10 business days. Your Vanto Connect access ends when the refund is issued.

If you think a charge is wrong, please contact us before raising a dispute or chargeback with your bank — we can usually resolve it faster directly.
