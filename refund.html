Refund & Cancellation Policy

When you can get a refund for a Vanto Connect founding license, when you can’t, and how to ask.

Effective 23 November 2026 Refund window 7 days Support: info@orvantolabs.in

On this page

  1. What this covers
  2. When you can get a refund
  3. When a refund isn’t available

  6. Cancellation
  7. Contact

In short

  • 7-day refund window if technical onboarding cannot be completed.
  • Non-refundable once Zoho/Meta workspace activation has been delivered.
  • Founding licenses are one-time payments, so there is no recurring charge to cancel.
  • Support and refund requests: info@orvantolabs.in.

01What this covers

This policy applies to Vanto Connect founding licenses sold by Orvanto Labs:

LicensePriceBilling
Global Founding License$399 USDOne-time payment
India Founding License₹14,999 INROne-time payment

Vanto Connect is delivered digitally through technical onboarding and activation in your Zoho CRM workspace. Custom Zoho Suite projects and other Orvanto Labs services are covered by the payment and cancellation terms in your written proposal.

02When you can get a refund

You can request a full refund within 7 days of purchase if technical onboarding cannot be completed — for example, if a technical issue prevents Vanto Connect from being connected to your Zoho CRM organization or your Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API account, and we are unable to resolve it.

Before approving a refund, we will work with you to try to complete onboarding. If it can’t be completed, and you asked within 7 days of purchase, you receive the full license fee back.

03When a refund isn’t available

  • After activation: once Zoho/Meta workspace activation has been delivered — meaning Vanto Connect is activated in your Zoho CRM workspace and connected to your Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API account — the license is non-refundable.
  • After 7 days: requests made more than 7 days after purchase.
  • Meta charges: WhatsApp conversation fees are billed by Meta directly to your Meta account, not by Orvanto Labs, so we cannot refund them. Contact Meta about those charges.
  • Policy violations: licenses suspended or terminated for breaching our Terms of Service, including the anti-spam rules.

04How to request a refund

  1. Email info@orvantolabs.in with the subject “Refund request”.
  2. Include your order ID, the email address used for the purchase, the purchase date, and a short description of the onboarding issue.
  3. We aim to reply within 24 hours and will confirm whether the refund is approved.

05How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method, in the original currency, through our payment provider. Once the refund is issued, the time it takes to appear depends on your bank or card issuer, and is typically 5–10 business days. Your Vanto Connect access ends when the refund is issued.

If you think a charge is wrong, please contact us before raising a dispute or chargeback with your bank — we can usually resolve it faster directly.

06Cancellation

Founding licenses are one-time payments with lifetime access, so there are no recurring license charges to cancel. You can stop using Vanto Connect at any time by uninstalling it from Zoho CRM and removing its access in your Meta Business settings. Stopping use after activation does not create a right to a refund.

To ask us to delete your data after you stop using the Product, follow the steps in our Privacy Policy.
